# Request · photographic mockups of the physical objects

The script-made photomontages (textiles and key ring) were discarded: they looked fake. In the documents the objects are shown with spec sheets (`print/spec-sheets/`). For photographic images we need a real shot, professional mockups (PSD with smart objects) or an image generator. Below are the ready requests, one per image.

Common rules: warm natural light of the Ligurian Riviera di Ponente, minimal styling, no people, no text besides the mark. The Incastro symbol is **satin-stitch embroidered** or **blind-debossed**, never printed. Symbol files: `assets/simbolo-vino.svg`, `assets/simbolo-crema.svg`. Colors: Vino #511B36, Crema #FFF3E7, Corallo #F25B45 (thin outline only).

1. **Towels, light version.** Neat stack of three high-quality cream terry towels (600 g/m²), woven band in the same tone. Symbol embroidered in Vino, 40 mm, centered 30 mm above the band on the folded front. Light wood or stone shelf, frontal framing slightly from above.
2. **Towels, dark version.** As above, Vino terry, cream embroidery.
3. **Bed, light version.** Made bed in cream-white percale, two pillowcases with 50 mm Oxford edge, Vino embroidered symbol 28 mm in the corner of the edge. Sheet turndown with 3 mm Corallo outline. Bright Ligurian room, simple headboard.
4. **Bed, dark version.** As above, Vino percale, cream embroidery, Corallo outline.
5. **Key ring.** Vegetable-tanned leather key ring in Vino, symbol shape, 40 × 50 mm, 4 mm thick, dyed edges, brushed brass ring with one key. Resting on light stone in the sun, sharp shadow.
6. **Embroidery detail.** Macro of the Vino symbol embroidered on cream linen, glossy satin stitch visible, raking light.

Recommended format: 3:2 landscape, at least 3000 px on the long side. Once ready, replace the sheets in plates 02, 14, 15 of the brand identity and in page 22 of the brand book (the sheets stay in the brand book as the production specification).
